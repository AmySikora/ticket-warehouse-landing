What Ticket VeriGuard checks

For assigned-seat tickets, Ticket VeriGuard compares identifying seat information in the listings you provide and looks for matching seats.

  • Event
  • Section
  • Row
  • Assigned seat or seat range

General admission, lawn tickets, and listings without complete section, row, and seat information are not flagged as duplicate assigned seats.

What a duplicate flag means

The same ticket inventory can legitimately appear on more than one resale marketplace. A duplicate flag does not prove fraud, counterfeiting, or that a ticket has already been sold.

Think of a duplicate as a signal to slow down and review the listings. Check the marketplace, seller information, seat details, price, and purchase protections before buying.

Likewise, if Ticket VeriGuard does not find a duplicate, that does not guarantee that a ticket is authentic, valid, or still available.
